# Security policy

Do not open public issues containing vulnerabilities, tokens, private user data, exploit payloads, or undisclosed abuse cases.

## Reporting

For the hackathon, report privately to the security-quality owner and team lead. Include affected version, reproducible steps, impact, and a safe proof of concept. Rotate exposed credentials immediately; preserve audit evidence without copying personal data.

## Scope

The threat model, controls, response playbook, AI-safety rules, and test plan are in [docs/04-security](docs/04-security/README.md).

No document can guarantee prevention of every cyberattack. The project uses risk reduction, defense in depth, least privilege, rapid detection, tested response, and recovery.
