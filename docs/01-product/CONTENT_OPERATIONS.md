# Content strategy and operations

## P0 library

Two flagship missions with deterministic evidence. If time permits, 6–12 shorter reviewed practice cases across source, context and citation skills. Quality/reproducibility outrank count.

## Lifecycle

Pitch/brief → source packet → evidence graph/accepted uncertainty → mission/hints/accessibility/media license → independent review → schema/eval/playtest → publish immutable version → monitor reports/expiry → correct with new version/archive.

## Editorial roles

Writer may draft; fact-check reviewer validates evidence; safety/accessibility reviewer validates harm/inclusion; publisher confirms gates. High-risk real-person/crisis/health/election cases need enhanced review or remain out of MVP.

## Localization

Localize learning objective, examples, social context, search/provider availability, laws and cultural risk—not word-for-word only. Ukrainian lived experience is design input, not trauma marketing.

## Sustainability

Documented scenario-pack format supports universities/youth organizations/fact-checkers. Contributions enter a review queue; partner status is never implied before agreement. Annual/new-event content refresh has owner/funding/review expiry.

## Copyright

Record license/use basis for every asset/excerpt/snapshot; prefer owned, open, public-domain or synthetic illustrative media. Links and factual metadata do not automatically authorize redistribution. Remove/replace assets when rights change.
