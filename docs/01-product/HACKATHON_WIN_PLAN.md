# UNESCO hackathon win plan

## Reality

The official deadline is **16 August 2026, 23:59 Paris time**. The written proposal is English-only, PDF/Word up to 10 MB; pitch video is at most three minutes. Four winners are selected against theme, clarity, innovation, feasibility/sustainability, and impact/inclusion.

## Jury mapping

| Criterion | Proof to show |
|---|---|
| Theme | learner acts as investigator; youth/community multiplier roadmap |
| Clarity | one sentence, one loop, one three-axis result screen |
| Innovation | process XP, calibration, Socratic coach, citation/provenance literacy |
| Feasibility | two deterministic cases, public standards/data, modular monolith, four owners |
| Sustainability | reviewed scenario-pack format, education/NGO distribution, measurable outcomes |
| Impact & inclusion | transfer metric, Ukrainian/English, accessible/low-bandwidth design |

## Six-day critical path

1. Freeze P0, brand, demo script and owner map.
2. Build vertical slice: mission → evidence → conclusion → receipt.
3. Polish two cases; complete AI eval and offline fallback.
4. Accessibility/security/QA pass; run micro-pilot.
5. Record demo, finish English proposal, compress evidence.
6. Submit early; retain checksum, confirmation and backup video link.

## What judges should remember

“Real media can still mislead.”

“Fluency is not evidence.”

“Detectors age. Verification habits transfer.”

## Claims discipline

Say `prototype`, `pilot signal`, `target`, or `planned partnership` accurately. Never claim production scale, partnerships, accuracy, or causal impact that has not been demonstrated.
