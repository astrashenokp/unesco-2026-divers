# Impact measurement

## North-star outcome

Percentage of learners who independently choose and execute an appropriate verification action on an unseen case one week later.

## Metric tree

| Outcome | Metric | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Better investigation | evidence-process rubric delta | not raw action count gaming |
| Better calibration | Brier score / calibration error before vs after | avoid rewarding blanket uncertainty |
| Transfer | unseen-format assessment | no training-case leakage |
| Retention | 7/30-day skill recall | streak is not learning evidence |
| Responsible sharing | justified share-choice rate | protect viewpoint neutrality |
| Inclusion | completion by device/language/accessibility cohort | no sensitive cohort under k-anonymity threshold |

## Hackathon pilot

- 8–20 participants in the target age group.
- Pre-test unseen case, 10–15 minute product session, post-test parallel case.
- Record task completion, evidence steps, confidence, qualitative confusion, and accessibility failures.
- Do not claim effectiveness from this sample; call it usability/feasibility evidence.
- Obtain consent and delete raw recordings/notes on schedule.

## Later evaluation

Randomize hint style or booster schedule only after ethics/privacy review. Pre-register primary metric, publish null/negative results, and keep learning outcomes separate from engagement.
