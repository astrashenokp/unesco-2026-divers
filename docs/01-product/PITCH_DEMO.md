# Three-minute pitch and demo

## Storyboard

**0:00–0:15 — Hook.** A dramatic viral image; learner trusts it at 82% confidence. “What if the image is real, but the story is false?”

**0:15–1:20 — Investigation.** Trace uploader → original publication → date/location mismatch. AI only asks “Who published this first?” Result: `Authentic × Contradicted × Misleading`. Learner changes confidence and earns evidence XP.

**1:20–2:05 — Citation hunt.** Paste a fluent AI answer, inspect its academic citation, discover DOI/title mismatch. “Fluency is not evidence.”

**2:05–2:30 — Learning proof.** Show skill path, confidence calibration and Evidence Receipt; mention lateral reading, spacing and unseen transfer measurement.

**2:30–2:48 — Scale/inclusion.** Ukrainian and English, low-bandwidth reviewed packs, teachers/youth organizations, privacy-safe outcomes.

**2:48–3:00 — Close.** “We are not building a machine that tells young people what to believe. We are training the reflex to investigate. Detectors age. Verification habits transfer.”

## Demo reliability

- bundle both cases and evidence locally;
- preload media and fonts;
- record a clean backup screen capture;
- disable notifications and unrelated accounts;
- rehearse at 2:45, leaving 15 seconds margin;
- show one live AI hint only if latency is predictable; deterministic hints remain the fallback.

## Submission checklist

- English proposal; ≤10 MB; readable on mobile and print.
- English pitch or accurate English subtitles.
- captions, contrast, no tiny text, sources in final frame/appendix.
- team members, problem, objective, audience, prototype, sustainability, creativity, feasibility.
- exact submission confirmation stored by two members.
