# User journeys

## Learner: first mission

1. Chooses language and accessibility preferences; may continue as guest.
2. Sees a two-screen promise: learn to investigate, AI will not decide for you.
3. Opens a viral-post mission and records instinct plus confidence.
4. Uses source/date/context actions; AI asks the next useful question.
5. Concludes `Authentic × Contradicted × Misleading`.
6. Updates confidence and selects `do not share / share with context / continue investigating`.
7. Receives process XP, one concrete learning insight, and an Evidence Receipt.

## Learner: citation hunt

1. Sees an AI answer citing a plausible paper.
2. Extracts title, authors, venue, year, DOI.
3. Queries curated metadata or a deterministic demo fixture.
4. Detects a nonexistent/mismatched citation.
5. Learns: fluent wording is not evidence.

## Returning learner

The path offers a short booster based on weak evidence skills and elapsed time. The learner can preserve a compassionate streak with a freeze; progression emphasizes mastery rather than punishment.

## Teacher/NGO facilitator — P1/P2

Selects a reviewed pack, shares an assignment code, and sees aggregated completion/skill distribution. They cannot view private prompts, browsing history, political profiles, or individual sensitive content.

## Failure and trust journeys

- External AI/search unavailable: switch to curated evidence; explain degraded mode.
- Evidence changed after publication: version the case, show correction, never rewrite history silently.
- User reports harm/error: acknowledge, quarantine case if severe, retain audit trail, provide appeal.
- AI refuses or is uncertain: offer deterministic hints and manual evidence steps.
