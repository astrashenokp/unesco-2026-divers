# Vision and strategy

## Vision

A generation that pauses, investigates, and communicates uncertainty responsibly before it amplifies information.

## Mission

Make high-quality verification practice accessible, memorable, measurable, and adaptable to local contexts.

## Strategic wedge

Win one narrow job first: a student completes a five-minute investigation and can explain why the evidence changed—or did not change—their confidence.

## Three horizons

### H1 — Hackathon proof

Two polished missions, one learning path, evidence actions, confidence before/after, nuanced result, Evidence Receipt, English/Ukrainian shell, and a measurable micro-pilot.

### H2 — Education pilot

Reviewed scenario packs, teacher assignment codes, cohort-level privacy-safe analytics, accessibility/low-bandwidth modes, and a four-week retention study.

### H3 — Platform

Partner authoring/review workflow, local packs, open scenario format, provenance adapters, research exports, and regional deployments. Extract services only after measured load or organizational ownership demands it.

## Moat

The moat is not a foundation model or detector. It is the combination of:

- high-quality versioned cases and evidence graphs;
- a validated process-scoring rubric;
- longitudinal calibration/transfer data;
- partner content-review network;
- trust earned through transparent uncertainty and corrections.

## Explicit non-goals

- universal truth engine;
- political source blacklist or one-dimensional credibility score;
- social network/leaderboard optimized for outrage;
- custom deepfake model in the hackathon MVP;
- unreviewed crowdsourced lessons;
- fully autonomous web agent with unrestricted browsing/actions.
