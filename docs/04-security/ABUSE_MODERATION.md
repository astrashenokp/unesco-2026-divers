# Abuse, content safety and moderation

## MVP safety choice

No public UGC, chat, debate room or unrestricted upload. Curated reviewed missions dramatically reduce risk. URL/media analysis remains post-MVP until the threat controls pass.

## Intake categories

Incorrect/outdated evidence; copyright/privacy; accessibility; hate/harassment/doxxing; graphic violence; sexual content/CSAM; self-harm; scam/malware; medical/election manipulation; prompt injection; impersonation/deepfake harm.

## Workflow

`report → triage → preserve minimal evidence → quarantine if urgent → reviewer decision → correction/removal → notify affected users when useful → appeal → close/audit`

## Service targets

- P0 imminent harm/CSAM/credential/malware: immediate automated quarantine and specialist/legal escalation; do not download/share more than necessary.
- P1 severe hate/doxxing/graphic/error with high reach: human review within hours.
- P2 ordinary accuracy/copyright/accessibility: review within two working days during pilot.

Targets require an actual staffed rotation before being promised publicly.

## Moderator safety

Blur by default, warnings, sanitized previews, minimum exposure, rotate workload, never require moderators to open unknown URLs/files on personal devices, restrict exports, and provide wellbeing escalation.

## Editorial neutrality

Apply the same evidence standard across viewpoints. Separate factual claim, opinion, satire and prediction. Record sources, reviewer, rationale and expiry. Offer correction/appeal and never use one universal source score.

## UGC launch gate

Age/safety/legal review, report/block/appeal, rate/reputation limits, quarantine/scanning, moderator tooling/staffing, transparency metrics, adversarial test, privacy retention and incident playbook must exist first.
