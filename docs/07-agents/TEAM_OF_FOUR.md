# Team of four programmers

## Role 1 — Frontend Experience Engineer

Owns Flutter learner app, design system, localization, accessibility, offline demo/pack cache and visual demo polish. Contract edge: generated API client and scenario presentation model.

## Role 2 — Platform & Data Engineer

Owns FastAPI/domain/Postgres, Firebase auth verification, Cloud Run/GCP/IaC, transactional completion/XP/outbox, provider runtime reliability. Contract edge: OpenAPI/events/storage.

## Role 3 — AI, Learning & Content Engineer

Owns evidence actions/adapters, Socratic policy/prompts, AI gateway/evals/fallback, mission fixtures/content quality, learning rubric and research claim discipline. Cannot publish sensitive content alone.

## Role 4 — Security, Quality & Release Engineer

Owns threat/privacy/control gates, CI/CD, test architecture, observability, release/runbooks, QA bot and submission integration. Coordinates product scope and release evidence but does not become the only tester.

## Daily integration

- A consumes mocked OpenAPI/fixtures from day one.
- B implements contract and golden transaction.
- C supplies two versioned packs and deterministic + AI coach behavior.
- D makes the golden flows executable in CI and demo rehearsal.
