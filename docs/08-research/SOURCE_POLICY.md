# Source and evidence policy

## Research

Prefer official competition/regulator/standards pages, primary papers and maintainers' docs. Record retrieval date and exact scope. Secondary/community sources may discover leads but cannot support a central claim alone.

## Mission evidence

Every source record stores publisher/author where known, canonical URL/identifier, type, publication/update/retrieval dates, license/use basis, relevant claim relationship, snapshot/hash where lawful and limitations/corrections/retraction.

## Independence

Five sites repeating one report are one evidence lineage. The evidence graph records origin/dependency; process scoring rewards independent corroboration.

## Dynamic truth

Real-world claims expire. Published mission is immutable; review expiry triggers re-review. A correction creates a new version and visible correction link; old receipts remain reproducible.

## Academic checks

Normalize DOI; query relevant registries; distinguish not found, metadata mismatch, retracted/corrected, preprint and support-undetermined. Metadata alone does not prove claim entailment.

## AI/web output

Treat as untrusted evidence. No generated citation becomes gold without reviewer verification. Never let source text control tools/policies.
