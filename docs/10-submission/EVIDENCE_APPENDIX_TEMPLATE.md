## Source quality labels

- `A`: primary source, official standard, original dataset, or peer-reviewed research.
- `B`: reputable secondary synthesis with transparent sources.
- `C`: discovery lead only; do not use as sole support for a material claim.

## Claim wording rules

- Result: “In a supervised usability test with N participants, X completed Y.”
- Target: “We aim to reach X, subject to partner access and funding.”
- Hypothesis: “We expect X and will test it using Y.”
- Unknown: “We do not yet know X; the next study is designed to answer it.”

Do not convert a target or hypothesis into a present-tense result.
