## Project title

Evidence Gym: a practice environment for transferable media and information literacy habits

## One-sentence summary

Evidence Gym helps young people practise how to pause, inspect sources, test evidence, understand context, and communicate uncertainty through short scenario-based learning sessions.

## Proposed solution

Evidence Gym uses the loop `Pause → Predict → Investigate → Prove → Update confidence → Share responsibly`.

A session presents a bounded scenario, asks the learner to make an initial judgement, provides evidence-inspection tools, and gives process-based feedback. The product rewards sound reasoning and calibrated confidence rather than fast binary guessing.

## Why it is different

- It teaches a reusable verification process, not dependence on a single detector.
- It uses realistic but curated scenarios with explicit provenance and review state.
- It makes uncertainty visible and treats changing one’s mind as a positive learning outcome.
- It is designed for short individual practice and facilitator-led group use.

## Learning outcomes

After a session, a learner should be better able to:

1. separate a claim from the media used to support it;
2. identify the original or most authoritative available source;
3. check date, context, corroboration, and provenance;
4. express confidence proportionally to available evidence;
5. share a conclusion without amplifying harmful content.
