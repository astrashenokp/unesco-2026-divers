## Additional requirements for minors

Before recruitment, document the applicable age threshold, assent procedure, guardian consent, safeguarding contact, mandatory-reporting process, school policy, and whether incentives are appropriate. Child assent and adult permission are separate; a young person’s refusal must be respected.
