# User interview script

Duration: 25–35 minutes. Goal: understand existing verification behaviour before pitching the solution.

## Moderator setup

- Record participant code, audience segment, date, moderator, consent state, and permitted recording mode.
- Do not record unnecessary age, school, political views, or identifiable examples.
- Remind the participant there are no right answers and they are testing the concept, not being tested.

## Opening

“We are exploring how people decide whether online information deserves trust. This is not an exam. You may skip any question or stop at any time.”

Confirm consent and recording choice before continuing.

## Current behaviour

1. Tell me about the last time an online post made you unsure.
2. What did you do next, if anything?
3. Which signals made it feel credible or suspicious?
4. When do you decide that checking is not worth the effort?
5. Who or what do you trust for help? Why?
6. How do you communicate when you are still uncertain?

Ask for concrete past behaviour. Avoid “Would you use…?” until after discovery questions.

## Concept reaction

Present the one-sentence concept without explaining all features.

1. What do you think this product would help you do?
2. Which part feels useful? Which part feels unnecessary?
3. What would make the exercise feel judgmental or unsafe?
4. When and where could a short practice session fit naturally?
5. What would make feedback trustworthy?

## Prototype tasks, if applicable

Use neutral prompts:

- “What would you do next?”
- “What do you expect this control to do?”
- “Please think aloud.”
- “What, if anything, changed your confidence?”

Do not teach the interface during a measured task. If help is required, record the help level.

## Close

1. What is the one thing you would change?
2. Who should not use this without support?
3. Is there anything important we did not ask?

Explain how data will be handled, repeat the withdrawal contact, and provide any promised debrief.
