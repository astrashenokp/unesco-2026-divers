# Content correction and withdrawal policy

## Purpose

Evidence Gym must model the behaviour it teaches: acknowledge uncertainty, correct errors transparently, and preserve provenance.

## Intake

Accept correction reports through a visible channel. Capture only:

- affected scenario and version;
- alleged issue and supporting source;
- severity/safety concern;
- optional reply contact and consent to retain it.

Do not require a reporter to create a public account or disclose unnecessary identity.

## Triage levels

| Level | Example | Initial action |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | active harm, exposed personal data, illegal content, dangerous instruction | immediately unpublish/restrict and escalate |
| High | materially wrong answer, fabricated source, missing rights | pause distribution and review urgently |
| Medium | misleading wording, incomplete context, broken evidence | flag and correct in the next controlled release |
| Low | typo, non-material presentation issue | normal editorial queue |

## Correction process

1. Acknowledge and assign a report ID.
2. Preserve the reported version and evidence privately where lawful/safe.
3. Verify against primary or authoritative sources.
4. Decide: no change, annotation, correction/new version, restriction, or withdrawal.
5. Record rationale and affected learning/analytics data.
6. Publish a plain-language correction note when users may have relied on the error.
7. Notify relevant partners and remove cached/distributed copies when feasible.
8. Review whether claims, rubrics, model prompts, metrics, and training materials also require correction.

## Integrity rules

- Never silently overwrite a published material claim.
- Do not delete good-faith criticism merely because it is uncomfortable.
- Protect reporters from retaliation and do not expose their identity.
- A withdrawn scenario cannot return without a new review and version.
- Learner history may retain the version ID, but should not continue presenting withdrawn content as valid.

## Service targets

Targets are set only after operating capacity is known. Until then, promise acknowledgement and response times conservatively; critical safety/privacy issues receive immediate containment priority.
