# Scenario content review checklist

Every scenario version must pass this checklist before publication.

## Learning purpose

- [ ] The target verification habit is explicit.
- [ ] The scenario cannot be solved reliably by a superficial visual clue alone.
- [ ] The evidence supports more than a lucky binary guess.
- [ ] Feedback explains process and uncertainty.
- [ ] The difficulty and expected prior knowledge are documented.

## Factual integrity and provenance

- [ ] Every claim and media asset has a source and access date.
- [ ] The original or most authoritative available source was sought.
- [ ] Dates, location, authorship, edits, and context were checked.
- [ ] Independent corroboration is recorded where relevant.
- [ ] Unknowns and disputes are represented honestly.
- [ ] The evidence snapshot and link-rot fallback are preserved appropriately.

## Rights and attribution

- [ ] License/permission permits the intended use and territory.
- [ ] Attribution, modification notice, and source-link requirements are satisfied.
- [ ] People’s privacy, publicity, and consent interests were considered separately from copyright.
- [ ] Brand/trademark use does not imply endorsement.
- [ ] Synthetic or team-created material is labelled when necessary.

## Safety and inclusion

- [ ] The learning benefit outweighs the risk of amplification.
- [ ] Graphic, hateful, targeted, or personally identifying content is excluded, redacted, warned, or access-limited.
- [ ] No participant or private individual can be doxxed through the evidence.
- [ ] The scenario avoids stereotyping and unnecessary partisan framing.
- [ ] Language, reading level, alt text, captions, keyboard order, and contrast are reviewed.
- [ ] A supervised-only or not-for-publication state is available.

## Product and measurement

- [ ] Expected answer/rubric accepts justified uncertainty where appropriate.
- [ ] Feedback cannot cite evidence absent from the versioned pack.
- [ ] Analytics identifiers contain no source secrets or participant data.
- [ ] The scenario is tagged for version, language, region, review state, and expiry/review date.
- [ ] A correction/withdrawal contact is visible.

## Sign-off record

Record scenario/version, reviewer perspectives, date, evidence bundle, unresolved concerns, allowed audience, next review date, and publication decision. A checkbox without a traceable record is not approval.
