# Governance model

## Scope

This model governs decisions, evidence, content publication, research, partnerships, spending, and risk. It intentionally does not define programming roles.

## Decision classes

| Class | Examples | Required record |
|---|---|---|
| Reversible routine | copy clarification, low-risk workshop adjustment | task/change note |
| Product direction | audience, scope, success measure, pricing principle | product decision log |
| Architecture/interface | system boundary, storage, public contract | ADR and contract review |
| High-risk | minor data, harmful content, public impact claim, security exception | written risk/evidence review and explicit approval |
| External commitment | partnership, grant condition, spend, public promise | agreement/decision record and exit terms |

## Cadence

- Weekly evidence review: new findings, assumptions invalidated, claim changes.
- Weekly content review while authoring: provenance, rights, safety, pedagogy, correction state.
- Before every external demo/submission: claim and privacy reconciliation.
- Monthly sustainability review after pilot: cash/runway, partner pipeline, operating burden.
- Incident-driven review: immediate when safety, privacy, security, or factual integrity is affected.

## Decision quality test

A material decision is ready when:

1. the user/outcome affected is named;
2. evidence and uncertainty are visible;
3. viable alternatives were considered;
4. safety, inclusion, privacy, rights, cost, and reversibility were checked;
5. the success/stop threshold and review trigger are defined;
6. downstream documents and claims are identified.

## Escalation

Stop the affected activity when there is ambiguous consent, suspected personal-data exposure, unsafe content, missing media rights, a material factual error, an undisclosed conflict of interest, or a promise beyond current authority/capacity.

Resume only after the issue, containment, decision, correction, and residual risk are recorded.

## Records and access

- Public documentation contains principles, approved claims, and non-sensitive decisions.
- Restricted research, incident, contract, and participant records live outside the public repository with least-privilege access and retention dates.
- A link from the repository may identify a record system and ID, but must not leak the restricted content.
