# Legal and policy readiness checklist

This is an issue-spotting checklist, not legal advice. Obtain qualified advice for launch jurisdictions, work with minors, research publication, employment/contracting, and material partnerships.

## Entity and authority

- [ ] Identify the submitting/operating person or entity and authority to accept terms.
- [ ] Record team IP ownership and contributor agreements.
- [ ] Check hackathon rules for eligibility, publicity, IP, prizes, and post-event obligations.
- [ ] Avoid public commitments that no authorised entity can fulfil.

## Privacy and data protection

- [ ] Map collected data, purposes, lawful basis/consent, recipients, storage locations, retention, and deletion.
- [ ] Complete age/jurisdiction analysis and child safeguarding review.
- [ ] Publish accurate privacy information and contact process.
- [ ] Contract and assess processors/vendors before real personal data is shared.
- [ ] Support access, correction, deletion, withdrawal, and incident procedures as applicable.
- [ ] Avoid sensitive inference and behavioural advertising.

## Content and intellectual property

- [ ] Inventory code, copy, media, datasets, models, fonts, prompts, and trademarks.
- [ ] Verify each item’s license/permission and attribution requirements.
- [ ] Distinguish repository license from third-party asset/API/data rights.
- [ ] Document transformative/editing choices and source provenance.
- [ ] Maintain takedown/correction procedure.

## Consumer, education, and AI claims

- [ ] Claims are evidence-linked and not misleading.
- [ ] AI limitations and human-review boundaries are explained.
- [ ] The product does not imply official UNESCO, school, source, or partner endorsement without permission.
- [ ] Accessibility statements describe tested scope, not absolute compliance.
- [ ] Learning and safety claims distinguish research stages and limitations.

## Contracts and partners

- [ ] Define scope, data roles, content rights, brand use, confidentiality, support, security, termination, and deletion/return.
- [ ] Record who owns newly created scenarios, translations, and research outputs.
- [ ] Ensure exit/fallback does not strand learners or expose data.
- [ ] Review export/sanctions, procurement, insurance, tax, and grant restrictions where applicable.

## Pre-launch legal gate

Create a dated record of applicable jurisdictions, counsel/reviewer, unresolved risks, accepted mitigations, documents published, vendor agreements, and explicit go/no-go decision.
