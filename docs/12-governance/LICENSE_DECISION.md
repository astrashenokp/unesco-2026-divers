# License decision record

No project license should be inferred from dependencies, reference repositories, a hackathon submission, or public visibility. The rights holder must choose deliberately before public reuse is invited.

## Inputs to decide

- Is the intended outcome open source, source-available, private, or dual-licensed?
- Which parts differ: software, documentation, scenario content, datasets, brand assets, research instruments?
- Must partners contribute improvements back?
- Can third parties use content commercially, translate it, or create derivatives?
- Are there minors, participant contributions, licensed media, or partner materials that cannot be relicensed?
- Do incoming-contribution terms match outgoing rights?

## Candidate families to assess with counsel/rights holders

| Asset | Candidate direction | Key question |
|---|---|---|
| Original software | permissive or copyleft open-source license | desired downstream obligations and ecosystem compatibility |
| Original documentation | permissive documentation/content license | attribution and derivative requirements |
| Original educational scenarios | Creative Commons family or reserved rights | commercial use, adaptation, share-alike, safety/context integrity |
| Brand/logo | usually separate trademark policy | prevent implied endorsement and confusing forks |
| Research data | often not publishable as raw data | consent, anonymisation, re-identification, dataset rights |

## Decision checklist

- [ ] Rights inventory complete.
- [ ] Team/contributor ownership documented.
- [ ] Third-party incompatibilities reviewed.
- [ ] Content/data/brand exceptions identified.
- [ ] License text added by an authorised rights holder.
- [ ] README and contribution terms match the decision.
- [ ] Third-party notices and source/attribution files are complete.

Until this decision is accepted, “all rights reserved unless otherwise stated” may apply by default depending on jurisdiction; do not tell contributors or users they have rights that have not been granted.
