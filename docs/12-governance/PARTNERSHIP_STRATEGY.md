# Partnership strategy

## Partnership thesis

Partners should strengthen one of four capabilities: access to learners, subject-matter/content review, safeguarding/research quality, or responsible distribution. A logo without a concrete contribution is not a partnership outcome.

## Priority partner profiles

| Profile | Value exchange | First pilot ask | Red flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| School/youth organisation | safe access and facilitator insight; receives structured learning activity | one supervised cohort and feedback session | forced participation, unclear consent authority |
| MIL/fact-checking organisation | expertise and reviewed cases; receives reusable learning format/insights | review two scenarios and rubric | pressure to promote partisan/brand conclusions |
| University/research group | study-design quality; receives defined research collaboration | protocol review and pilot-method advice | publication rights conflict, inappropriate data demand |
| Accessibility organisation | lived-experience review; receives compensated influence on design | accessibility walkthrough | tokenistic unpaid consultation |
| Foundation/public-interest funder | resources and convening; receives transparent milestones | fund bounded pilot and evaluation | control over editorial outcomes or learner data |

## Qualification questions

1. What outcome do we share, and how will we know it happened?
2. What does each side contribute, by when, and at what cost?
3. Who has authority for participants, consent, content, brand, and publication?
4. What data/content crosses the boundary and under which rights?
5. What safeguarding, accessibility, security, and incident duties apply?
6. What happens if the pilot fails, ends early, or a correction is needed?

## Pilot structure

- One audience and setting.
- One learning-loop hypothesis.
- Small reviewed scenario set.
- Predeclared measures and stop conditions.
- Named facilitator and support channel.
- No expansion until the joint retrospective and decision record.

## Pipeline stages

`identified → qualified → discovery held → pilot brief agreed → legal/safety ready → active → evaluated → renew/close`

Track next action, decision date, dependency, and reason for loss. Do not collect unrelated contact intelligence.

## Independence safeguards

- Partners cannot buy favourable learning conclusions.
- Sponsorship is disclosed where relevant.
- Scenario corrections remain possible after partner approval.
- Learner data is not exchanged for access.
- Brand use requires explicit permission and cannot imply UNESCO or institutional endorsement.
