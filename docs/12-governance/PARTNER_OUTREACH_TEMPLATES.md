# Partner outreach templates

Personalise every message. Do not send bulk outreach with unsupported claims.

## Initial email

**Subject:** Small supervised media-literacy pilot with [organisation]

Hello [name],

We are building Evidence Gym, a short scenario-based practice environment that teaches young people to inspect sources, evidence, context, and confidence rather than rely on a “truth detector”.

Your work on [specific programme/public contribution] suggests a concrete fit. We would like to explore a bounded pilot with [audience]: [number/range] supervised sessions, [duration], a reviewed scenario set, and a short evaluation. We can provide [materials/support]; we would ask [specific contribution].

This is currently [prototype/pilot state]. We are not claiming validated long-term impact; the purpose of the pilot is to test [question]. We can share the concept, safety/privacy approach, and pilot protocol before any commitment.

Would a 25-minute discovery conversation during [date range] be useful?

[Name and verifiable contact]
