# Project board operating template

## Board columns

`Inbox → Ready → In progress → In review/validation → Blocked → Done`

## Work item fields

- Stable ID and concise outcome.
- User/problem affected.
- Acceptance evidence.
- Scope and explicit non-goals.
- Dependencies and external deadline.
- Risk labels: safety, privacy, security, rights, accessibility, claim, partner.
- Source document/decision link.
- Current next action and review date.
- Final evidence link and follow-up.

## Entry criteria for Ready

- Outcome and acceptance conditions are understood.
- Required decision/contract exists.
- Dependencies are available or scheduled.
- High-risk review is identified.
- Work is small enough to validate without a hidden multi-week chain.

## Done criteria

“Done” means the intended evidence exists, affected documentation/claims are updated, known limitations are recorded, and any follow-up has a separate item. Activity alone is not completion.

## Suggested views

- Submission critical path.
- Pilot readiness and safeguards.
- Content review queue and expiry.
- Open decisions and evidence debt.
- Partner commitments and deadlines.
- Risks due within 14 days.

## WIP rule

Limit parallel work. When an item is blocked, record the exact missing decision/input and review date; move effort to the next ready outcome rather than accumulating partially finished work.
