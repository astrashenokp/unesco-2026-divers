# Debrief question bank

Choose a small set; a debrief is not another quiz.

## Notice the process

- What was your first assumption?
- Which step did you almost skip?
- What evidence was relevant, and what was merely vivid?
- Did several sources add independence, or repeat one origin?

## Calibrate confidence

- What would justify 90% confidence rather than 60%?
- What changed your confidence most?
- What evidence would make you revise again?
- Is the conclusion “supported”, “contradicted”, or “not enough evidence”?

## Understand media and context

- Can authentic media support a false claim? How?
- What do date, location, crop, caption, and publication history change?
- What does missing provenance tell us—and what does it not prove?

## Reflect on AI and tools

- Was a tool result evidence, a clue, or a verdict?
- How could the tool be wrong or incomplete?
- Could you reproduce the conclusion without trusting one provider?

## Share responsibly

- How can we explain the finding without repeating the harmful claim?
- Who could be affected by sharing the example?
- When is it better not to repost, even to debunk?
- What context and uncertainty should accompany a correction?

## Transfer

- Where else could this same verification step help?
- What changes when the content is breaking news, satire, advertising, or academic citation?
- What is your personal “pause trigger” for the next week?
