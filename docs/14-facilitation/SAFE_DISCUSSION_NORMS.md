# Safe discussion norms

Introduce and adapt these norms with participants.

1. We examine claims and evidence, not the intelligence or identity of the person who believed or shared something.
2. We can say “I do not know” and change our minds without losing status.
3. We do not repeat harmful claims more than needed to analyse them.
4. We do not search for, reveal, or guess private information about real people.
5. We distinguish what the evidence shows from what we infer.
6. We challenge ideas with reasons and allow others time to think.
7. We may pause, skip, or leave an activity.
8. We report factual, rights, safety, or accessibility concerns through the correction route.

## Facilitator intervention phrases

- “Let’s return to the claim and evidence, not the person.”
- “We do not need to display or repeat that harmful material to continue.”
- “That detail could identify someone; please do not share it here.”
- “We have reached the limit of the evidence. What uncertainty remains?”
- “We are pausing this scenario and will review it before reuse.”
