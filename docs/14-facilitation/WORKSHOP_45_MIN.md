# 45-minute Evidence Gym workshop

## Outcome

Participants practise a repeatable verification loop and can name one evidence-based reason for updating confidence.

## Agenda

| Time | Activity | Facilitation evidence |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Purpose, opt-out, discussion norms | Participants know this is practice, not a test of intelligence |
| 5–9 min | “Claim vs media vs context” micro-example | Group can distinguish the three |
| 9–21 min | Scenario 1 individually/pairs | Initial confidence and investigation steps captured if consented |
| 21–28 min | Evidence comparison and debrief | Participants explain which source changed their view |
| 28–38 min | Scenario 2: new format/topic | Transfer attempt without giving away the prior answer pattern |
| 38–43 min | Responsible sharing exercise | Group rewrites a conclusion without harmful amplification |
| 43–45 min | Exit reflection and support/correction route | One takeaway and unresolved question |

## Materials

- reviewed scenario/version identifiers;
- accessible digital or printed fallback;
- source/evidence cards;
- confidence scale that includes uncertainty;
- content warning and private stop/help mechanism;
- facilitator correction/report form.

## Exit prompts

1. Which step will you reuse outside this workshop?
2. What evidence most changed—or should have changed—your confidence?
3. When is “not enough evidence” the responsible answer?

## Adaptation rules

Shorten by removing a scenario, not the safety framing or debrief. For younger or unfamiliar audiences, reduce source volume and reading load without simplifying the conclusion into a magic detector. For remote delivery, provide a private help/stop channel and test screen-reader/keyboard access.
